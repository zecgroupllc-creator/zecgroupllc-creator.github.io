Privacy Policy

App: Dewveil Relic (Android package com.du23u3077548)
Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Dewveil Relic ("the game", "the app", "we") handles information.

Information the game collects

The game itself does not collect, store or share any personal information. You do not need an account to play, and the game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your level progress and best scores are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve, personalize and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • app interaction data, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostics, such as crash and performance information related to ads.

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. To learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. You can review or change your choice at any time with the "Privacy options" button on the app's About screen.

How to opt out of personalized ads

On most Android devices you can limit ad personalization or remove your Advertising ID:

  • Open Settings > Google > Ads (on some devices Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Tap Delete advertising ID to stop apps from using it for personalized ads.

You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Security and sale of data

Data sent by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit. We do not sell personal data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
